# Delete your account and data — {{BRAND}}

This page explains how to delete your data from the **{{BRAND}}** app for Android, iPhone and iPad (controller: {{COMPANY_NAME}}).

## In the app

1. Open {{BRAND}} and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete my data** and confirm. This works for guest profiles and for accounts connected with Google.
3. Access is revoked at once and your data is deleted from our servers; the app also clears the data on the phone. Uninstalling the app on its own does not delete server data.

## Without the app

E-mail **{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}** with the subject "Delete account", from your account's address if you signed in. We will confirm the deletion.

## What is deleted and what is kept

- Your profile, tradition, completed days, progress, reminder devices and link to your Google account are deleted.
- To delete only your tradition and keep the account: **Settings → Tradition** → "Not sure".
- Pseudonymous usage data already recorded is not deleted item by item: it expires automatically after 35 days. The links between your account and that data are deleted at once. Aggregated statistics contain no account identifier.
- An anonymous digest of the purchase receipt, without your account or history, may remain to prevent duplicate claims. The App Store and Google Play keep their own purchase records.
- Deleting the account or uninstalling does not cancel the subscription. Cancel renewal separately in the App Store or Google Play.
