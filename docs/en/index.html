Your calendar

Every day counts

Your calendar shows how you're doing. If you miss a day, the passage isn't lost: it waits for you in Catch-up until you have a moment.

In step with the year

The right passage for every day

Each day's passage follows the Christian calendar of your tradition: the birth of Jesus at Christmas, the Passion in Holy Week, the Resurrection at Easter. Everyone in your tradition reads the same passage on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What if I can't make it one day?

Nothing is lost. The passage stays in Catch-up and in the Archive, and you can complete it whenever you like. Your calendar shows it in orange if you do it the next day, or in grey if later.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start without signing up. If you sign in, your progress is saved and synced across your devices.

How much does it cost?

The first 7 days are free. Then you choose a monthly or annual subscription; you'll see the price for your country in the app before you confirm. It renews automatically until you cancel it from the App Store or Google Play.

What do you do with my data?

Only what the app needs to work. Your tradition is stored only with your consent, it is never used for advertising and we never sell your data. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

From Settings, inside the app. We also explain how to do it without the app.

How can I contact you?

Write to hola@biblive.app. A person will reply.
