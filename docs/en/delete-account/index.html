Delete your account and data

Last updated: 28 October 2026

On this page
  • In the app
  • Without the app
  • What is deleted and what is kept

This page explains how to delete your data from the Biblive app (Google Play, developer Daniel Amador Montaño).

In the app

  1. Open Biblive and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm. This applies to guest profiles and accounts connected with Google.
  3. The service revokes access and queues resumable account deletion; the app clears local data after the request is accepted. Uninstalling alone does not request server deletion.

Without the app

E-mail privacidad@biblive.app from your account's address with the subject "Delete account". We will confirm the deletion.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Account-linked profile/tradition, completed days, progress, reminder devices and Google account binding are removed by the deletion worker. Failed cleanup steps retry; the privacy contact handles unresolved requests. A one-day revocation marker prevents old credentials from being reused.
  • You can delete only your tradition without deleting the account: in Settings → Tradition, choose "Not sure".
  • Raw pseudonymous analytics rows already written to Parquet expire under the configured 35-day S3 lifecycle; they are not selectively deleted. Account-to-analytics/campaign mappings are deleted. Aggregated statistics contain no account identifier. A purchase-token digest may remain without the former account or history to prevent duplicate ownership; Google Play retains its own purchase records.
  • Deleting the app account or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel renewal separately in Google Play.
