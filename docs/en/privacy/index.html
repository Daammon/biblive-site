This policy covers the Biblive app for Android, iPhone and iPad ("the app") and this website.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Daniel Amador Montaño (NIF 29202270K), Calle de Braille 18, 28034 Madrid, Spain. Privacy contact: privacidad@biblive.app.

2. Summary

  • The app shows one Bible passage a day as a film, audio and text. The words are the Bible text, unchanged.
  • Your tradition (for example Catholic or Protestant) reveals your religious beliefs. We only record it if you give explicit consent on the tradition screen. You can choose "Not sure" and we record no tradition.
  • We never use your tradition for advertising, never share it with advertising or analytics companies, and never sell your data. The app shows no ads.
  • Analytics, crash reports and marketing measurement run only if you agree.
  • Account, calendar and progress data are hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party code.

3. Your profile and account

When you start, the app creates a guest profile and keeps your preferences, completed days and progress on the phone and on our servers. On Android you can sign in with Google to recover and sync your data. Uninstalling the app removes data on the device but does not delete the server account or cancel a subscription; to delete the account use Settings → Delete my data or follow the steps to delete your account. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backups.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you request deletion. Deletion revokes access immediately and removes linked data. An anonymous digest of the purchase receipt may remain to prevent duplicate claims; it contains neither your account nor your history.
  • Pseudonymous usage events: deleted automatically after 35 days. If you withdraw consent, we stop collecting them and remove the links between your account and analytics; events already recorded expire after 35 days. Aggregated statistics contain no account identifier and leave out groups smaller than ten people.
  • Campaign links: 35 days, or earlier if you withdraw consent or delete the account.
  • Crash reports (Android): Google starts removing them after 90 days. Withdrawing consent stops new collection.
  • Problem reports: 90 days. E-mails you send us: as long as needed to help you.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can export your calendar and request account deletion in the app. Write to privacidad@biblive.app. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14. If you are under 14, you need the consent of a parent or guardian to use it. If you believe a child under 14 has given us their data without that consent, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest; access is limited to the people and systems that need it.

10. Changes

We will publish any change on this page with a new date and tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
